Abstract

Connected Cardiovascular Intelligence (CCI) is presented as a clinician-centered, longitudinal, multimodal framework for integrating and interpreting cardiovascular information while preserving temporal relationships, clinical context, provenance, data quality, intervention history, uncertainty, and human clinical oversight.

The framework conceptualizes the patient as an evolving clinical trajectory rather than a collection of isolated observations or episodic snapshots. Artificial intelligence and machine learning are positioned as computational components within a broader governed clinical-intelligence architecture rather than as independent clinical authorities.
