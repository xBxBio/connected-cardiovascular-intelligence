Abstract

Longitudinal Multimodal Cardiovascular Patient-State Representation presents a public scientific framework for representing cardiovascular health as an evolving longitudinal patient state rather than as isolated measurements or episodic encounters.

The framework emphasizes temporal coherence, multimodal evidence integration, provenance, uncertainty, clinical context, intervention history, and clinician interpretability. It addresses how electrocardiographic, imaging, laboratory, hemodynamic, medication, wearable, remote-monitoring, genomic, procedural, and other clinically relevant data can contribute to a coherent representation of cardiovascular state across time.
