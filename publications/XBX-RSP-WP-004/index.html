Abstract

Validation Framework for Multimodal Cardiovascular AI presents a public scientific framework for validating artificial intelligence systems that combine multiple cardiovascular data modalities, including electrocardiographic waveforms, cardiovascular imaging, hemodynamics, laboratory measurements, medications, clinical narratives, wearable and implantable-device data, genomic information, and longitudinal outcomes.

The framework treats validation as a layered evidence system organized around intended use and claims. It distinguishes analytical performance, clinical validity, external generalizability, human-factors performance, clinical utility, software and cybersecurity assurance, safety engineering, and lifecycle monitoring.
