# Contributing to xBxBio

## Welcome

Thank you for your interest in contributing to **xBxBio Connected Cardiovascular Intelligence™**.

xBxBio supports thoughtful public collaboration and professional dialogue across cardiovascular informatics, clinical AI, interoperability, digital health, data integrity, quality, regulatory science, healthcare technology, and related disciplines.

This repository contains **selected public and non-proprietary materials**.

Contributions should support responsible technical, scientific, educational, and professional collaboration while protecting patient privacy, security, confidentiality, intellectual property, and scientific integrity.

---

## Before You Contribute

Before submitting any material, ask:

> **Do I have the right to make this information publicly available?**

If the answer is uncertain, **do not submit it**.

Public GitHub repositories can be viewed, indexed, copied, referenced, downloaded, or retained by others.

Contributors are responsible for ensuring that materials they submit are appropriate for unrestricted public disclosure.

---

## Appropriate Contributions

Depending on the context and current scope of the repository, appropriate contributions may include:

* Corrections to public documentation
* Clarifications
* Public technical perspectives
* Educational improvements
* Public interoperability observations
* Suggestions concerning clinical-AI governance
* Data-integrity perspectives
* Publicly available standards-related observations
* References to relevant public scientific literature
* Non-confidential technical diagrams
* Accessibility improvements
* Documentation improvements
* Typographical corrections
* Constructive questions
* Public research perspectives
* Suggestions for improving clarity or usability

Submission of a contribution does not guarantee that it will be accepted, incorporated, published, or maintained.

---

## Do Not Submit Patient Information

**Do not submit real patient information, patient-identifiable information, or protected health information (PHI).**

Do not submit:

* Patient names
* Medical-record numbers
* Patient account numbers
* Patient email addresses
* Patient telephone numbers
* Full dates of birth
* Identifiable medical records
* Identifiable medical images
* Screenshots containing patient information
* Device identifiers linked to identifiable patients
* Patient credentials
* Geographic or demographic information that could reasonably identify an individual
* Any other information that could directly or indirectly identify a patient

Clinical examples should use **fictional or appropriately authorized non-identifiable information** and must not permit identification of an individual.

Removing a patient's name alone does not necessarily make clinical information appropriate for public disclosure.

When uncertain:

**Do not submit the information.**

---

## Do Not Submit Confidential Information

Do not contribute information that you do not have the right to disclose publicly.

This includes:

* Employer-confidential information
* Customer-confidential information
* Partner-confidential information
* Trade secrets
* Non-public research
* Non-public product information
* Confidential regulatory information
* Contractually restricted information
* Confidential technical documentation
* Internal business information
* Confidential healthcare-organization information
* Unreleased intellectual property

If information was obtained through employment, consulting, research, partnership, healthcare operations, contractual relationships, or another restricted environment, ensure that public disclosure is appropriately authorized before submitting it.

---

## Do Not Submit Proprietary xBxBio Information

This repository does **not** invite disclosure of proprietary xBxBio implementation details.

Unless specifically authorized for public release, do not submit or disclose:

* Proprietary xBxBio source code
* Proprietary algorithms
* Proprietary AI/ML implementations
* Internal architecture
* Security architecture
* Infrastructure configuration
* Internal development information
* Confidential technical specifications
* Credentials or secrets
* Protected intellectual property
* Non-public research and development information

Public xBxBio materials describe selected principles, perspectives, and appropriately releasable information.

They do not represent the complete proprietary xBxBio platform or implementation.

---

## Security Information

**Do not report security vulnerabilities through public Issues, Discussions, comments, or pull requests.**

Do not publicly submit:

* Passwords
* API keys
* Access tokens
* Private keys
* Authentication credentials
* Security secrets
* Exploit information that could create unnecessary risk
* Sensitive configuration information
* Non-public infrastructure information
* Security architecture
* Information that could facilitate unauthorized access

Potential security concerns should be reported according to the repository's **SECURITY.md** policy.

Security reports for xBxBio should be directed to:

**[ken@xbxbio.com](mailto:ken@xbxbio.com)**

Use a subject line similar to:

**Security Report — xBxBio**

## Scientific and Clinical Integrity

Contributions involving scientific, clinical, or healthcare-technology claims should make reasonable efforts to preserve scientific integrity.

Contributors should:

* Represent evidence accurately
* Distinguish evidence from hypothesis
* Identify material uncertainty
* Avoid presenting speculation as established fact
* Avoid overstating preliminary findings
* Provide appropriate context
* Cite reliable public sources where practical for consequential claims
* Correct material errors when identified

Constructive disagreement is welcome.

**Misrepresentation of scientific or clinical evidence is not.**

---

## Responsible Clinical AI

Contributions involving artificial intelligence or machine learning in healthcare should recognize considerations such as:

* Intended use
* Data quality
* Data provenance
* Clinical context
* Temporal context
* Bias
* Generalizability
* Validation
* Performance limitations
* Uncertainty
* Human oversight
* Governance
* Monitoring
* Patient safety

AI-generated material should be reviewed before submission.

The use of an AI system does not remove the contributor's responsibility for the accuracy, appropriateness, legality, originality, confidentiality, and public suitability of submitted material.

**Human clinical judgment remains central to accountable healthcare decision-making.**

---

## Regulatory and Quality Information

Contributions discussing regulatory requirements, quality systems, standards, validation, data integrity, compliance, or certification should recognize that applicability can depend upon factors including:

* Intended use
* Technology classification
* Deployment model
* Jurisdiction
* Regulatory status
* Organizational responsibility
* Risk
* Applicable laws, regulations, standards, and guidance

A community contribution should not be presented as an official regulatory, legal, quality, compliance, certification, clearance, or authorization determination unless it is supported by an appropriate authoritative source and accurately characterized.

---

## Intellectual Property

Only contribute material that you have the right to submit publicly.

Do not knowingly submit:

* Unauthorized copyrighted material
* Trade secrets
* Proprietary third-party source code
* Restricted documentation
* Confidential algorithms
* Protected intellectual property belonging to another party

Submitting material to this repository does not automatically transfer ownership of intellectual property unless explicitly governed by an applicable agreement or license.

The absence of an open-source license should not be interpreted as granting unrestricted rights to xBxBio proprietary materials.

---

## Contributions Do Not Define the xBxBio Platform

Public contributions, suggestions, Discussions, Issues, or pull requests should not be interpreted as defining xBxBio's production architecture, product roadmap, regulatory status, clinical capabilities, proprietary technology, or future development commitments.

Acceptance of a contribution does not necessarily indicate that a concept, technology, feature, standard, workflow, or implementation is or will become part of an xBxBio product or platform.

---

## How to Contribute

Depending on the type of contribution, you may use:

### Discussions

Use Discussions for:

* Professional dialogue
* Technical questions
* Public research perspectives
* Ideas
* Conceptual discussion
* Interoperability topics
* Clinical-AI governance topics
* Public educational discussion

### Issues

Use Issues, when enabled and appropriate, for:

* Public documentation problems
* Reproducible issues involving publicly released repository materials
* Clearly scoped improvement suggestions

**Do not use Issues for confidential or security-sensitive information.**

### Pull Requests

Use pull requests, when appropriate, for proposed changes to public repository materials.

A pull request should:

* Have a clear purpose
* Be appropriately scoped
* Explain the proposed change
* Avoid unrelated modifications
* Contain only information appropriate for public disclosure
* Comply with this contribution policy
* Comply with the xBxBio Code of Conduct

---

## Review and Acceptance

xBxBio may review contributions for factors including:

* Relevance
* Accuracy
* Scientific integrity
* Technical quality
* Privacy
* Security
* Confidentiality
* Intellectual property
* Regulatory or quality implications
* Public suitability
* Repository scope
* Maintainability

xBxBio may accept, request modification of, decline, close, remove, or defer a contribution as appropriate.

No obligation exists to accept or implement a submitted contribution.

---

## Community Conduct

All contributors are expected to follow the repository's **CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md**.

Professional disagreement is welcome.

Harassment, threats, deliberate disruption, privacy violations, unauthorized disclosure, and misuse of the community are not.
