# xBxBio Community Code of Conduct

## Purpose

The xBxBio GitHub community supports professional, scientific, clinical, engineering, and technical dialogue related to **Connected Cardiovascular Intelligence™**, cardiovascular informatics, clinical AI, interoperability, digital health, data integrity, quality, regulatory science, and related healthcare technologies.

Participation in xBxBio public repositories and Discussions is expected to support an environment that is professional, evidence-conscious, constructive, secure, and appropriate for healthcare-related technical dialogue.

These requirements apply to discussions, comments, issues, pull requests, contributions, and other interactions within xBxBio public GitHub resources.

---

## 1. Professional Conduct

Participants are expected to:

* Communicate professionally and respectfully.
* Critique ideas, evidence, methods, and technologies rather than individuals.
* Support constructive technical and scientific disagreement.
* Clearly distinguish facts, evidence, hypotheses, opinions, and speculation.
* Avoid harassment, threats, intimidation, personal attacks, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
* Respect differences in professional expertise and experience.
* Contribute in a manner consistent with responsible scientific and professional dialogue.

Technical disagreement is welcome.

**Unprofessional conduct is not.**

---

## 2. Patient Privacy and Protected Health Information

**Do not post real patient information, patient-identifiable information, or protected health information (PHI) in this public repository or community.**

This includes information that could directly or indirectly identify an individual patient.

Do not post:

* Patient names
* Medical-record numbers
* Account numbers
* Patient email addresses
* Patient telephone numbers
* Full dates of birth
* Patient photographs containing identifying information
* Identifiable medical images
* Device identifiers linked to individuals
* Geographic or demographic information that could reasonably identify a patient
* Screenshots containing patient information
* Clinical records containing identifying information
* Any other information that could reasonably compromise patient privacy

**Clinical examples should use fictional or appropriately authorized non-identifiable information and must not permit identification of an individual.**

Removal of a patient's name alone does not necessarily make clinical information appropriate for public disclosure.

If there is uncertainty about whether information could identify a patient or is appropriate for unrestricted public disclosure:

**Do not post it.**

---

## 3. No Individual Medical Advice

xBxBio public GitHub resources are intended for technical, scientific, educational, and professional discussion.

They are **not a substitute for professional medical evaluation, diagnosis, or treatment**.

Do not use this community to:

* Request emergency medical assistance.
* Obtain a diagnosis for an identifiable individual.
* Request patient-specific treatment recommendations.
* Direct changes to an individual's prescribed therapy.
* Replace consultation with an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

Technical, scientific, or educational discussion of clinical topics does not constitute individual medical advice.

---

## 4. Confidential and Proprietary Information

Do not post information that you do not have the right to disclose.

This includes:

* Confidential company information
* Trade secrets
* Proprietary source code
* Proprietary algorithms
* Confidential model implementations
* Non-public internal architecture
* Non-public technical specifications
* Confidential research
* Non-public product information
* Customer information
* Partner information
* Contractually restricted information
* Confidential regulatory information
* Unreleased intellectual property

xBxBio public GitHub resources are **public environments**.

Assume that anything posted publicly may be viewed, indexed, copied, referenced, or retained by others.

---

## 5. xBxBio Intellectual Property

Public discussion of xBxBio concepts, technologies, research perspectives, or Connected Cardiovascular Intelligence™ does not constitute authorization to disclose, reproduce, implement, distribute, or otherwise use proprietary xBxBio technology.

Unless specifically authorized for public release, do not post:

* Proprietary xBxBio source code
* Proprietary algorithms
* Proprietary AI/ML implementations
* Detailed internal architecture
* Confidential system designs
* Security architecture
* Infrastructure configuration
* Confidential development information
* Credentials or secrets
* Protected intellectual property
* Confidential technical documentation
* Non-public research and development information

Public xBxBio materials describe **selected principles, perspectives, and appropriately releasable information** and should not be interpreted as disclosure of the proprietary implementation underlying the xBxBio platform.

---

## 6. Scientific and Clinical Integrity

Healthcare technology discussions can influence professional understanding and decision-making.

Participants should therefore make reasonable efforts to:

* Represent evidence accurately.
* Avoid intentionally misleading scientific or clinical claims.
* Identify material uncertainty.
* Distinguish demonstrated evidence from hypotheses or speculation.
* Provide appropriate context for technical claims.
* Avoid presenting unvalidated conclusions as established clinical facts.
* Correct material errors when identified.
* Cite reliable sources when making consequential scientific or clinical claims where practical.
* Avoid overstating the clinical significance of preliminary findings.

Scientific disagreement is legitimate.

**Misrepresentation of evidence is not.**

---

## 7. Responsible Artificial Intelligence Discussion

Discussion of artificial intelligence and machine learning should recognize the additional responsibilities associated with healthcare applications.

Relevant considerations may include:

* Intended use
* Data quality
* Data provenance
* Clinical context
* Temporal context
* Bias
* Generalizability
* Validation
* Performance limitations
* Uncertainty
* Human oversight
* Governance
* Monitoring
* Patient safety

AI-generated outputs should not automatically be treated as clinically authoritative merely because they were produced computationally.

A responsible clinical intelligence environment must consider not only whether a technology **can** generate an output, but whether the available evidence and context appropriately support its use.

**Human clinical judgment remains central to accountable healthcare decision-making.**

---

## 8. Security and Vulnerability Information

Do not publicly disclose information that could materially compromise the security of xBxBio, another organization, healthcare infrastructure, users, systems, or patients.

Do not post:

* Passwords
* API keys
* Access tokens
* Private keys
* Authentication credentials
* Exploitable infrastructure details
* Sensitive configuration information
* Patient-system access information
* Security secrets
* Information intended to facilitate unauthorized access

Potential security vulnerabilities should be handled responsibly and should not be publicly disclosed in a manner that unnecessarily increases risk.

Public issues or Discussions should **not** be used to publish sensitive vulnerability details when doing so could create a security risk.

---

## 9. Regulatory and Quality Discussions

Discussion of regulatory requirements, standards, quality systems, validation, data integrity, or compliance should recognize that applicability can depend upon factors including:

* Intended use
* Technology classification
* Deployment model
* Jurisdiction
* Regulatory status
* Organizational responsibilities
* Risk
* Applicable laws, regulations, standards, and guidance

Community statements should not automatically be interpreted as official regulatory, legal, quality, compliance, or certification determinations.

References to standards, regulations, frameworks, or quality principles do not by themselves establish compliance, certification, clearance, authorization, or approval.

---

## 10. Contributions

Contributions should be limited to information and materials that the contributor has the right to provide publicly.

Before contributing, participants are responsible for ensuring that their contribution does not knowingly contain:

* Patient-identifiable information
* Protected health information
* Confidential information
* Trade secrets
* Unauthorized proprietary information
* Credentials or secrets
* Malicious code
* Information subject to disclosure restrictions
* Unauthorized copyrighted material

Contributors should also avoid submitting information obtained from employers, clients, healthcare organizations, research organizations, partners, or other parties when public disclosure has not been appropriately authorized.

Submitting material does not automatically transfer ownership of intellectual property unless explicitly governed by an applicable agreement or license.

---

## 11. Public Repository Awareness

xBxBio public repositories and Discussions are publicly accessible.

Content may be visible to individuals and organizations outside the immediate xBxBio community and may be indexed by search engines or other services.

Before posting, ask:

> **Is this information appropriate for unrestricted public disclosure?**

If the answer is uncertain:

**Do not post it until appropriate authorization has been obtained.**

---

## 12. Responsible Use of Public xBxBio Information

The presence of information in an xBxBio public repository does not mean that every xBxBio technology, capability, architecture, model, workflow, or implementation is publicly disclosed.

Public information should not be used to infer that undisclosed proprietary implementation details are absent from the xBxBio platform.

Unless expressly stated otherwise, public materials should also not be interpreted as:

* Regulatory clearance or authorization
* Clinical validation of a particular functionality
* Commercial availability
* Approval for a specific clinical use
* A warranty of performance
* Medical advice
* Permission to use proprietary xBxBio intellectual property

Applicable requirements depend upon the specific technology, intended use, implementation, jurisdiction, and regulatory framework.

---

## 13. Moderation

xBxBio maintainers may moderate community content to protect the quality, security, professionalism, and appropriate use of xBxBio public resources.

Depending on the circumstances, moderation may include:

* Requesting clarification or correction
* Requesting removal of restricted information
* Editing or hiding content where platform functionality permits
* Removing inappropriate content
* Locking discussions
* Closing discussions or issues
* Restricting participation
* Reporting serious violations through appropriate channels

Content may be moderated when it creates concerns involving:

* Patient privacy
* Security
* Confidentiality
* Intellectual property
* Patient safety
* Scientific integrity
* Legal or regulatory obligations
* Professional conduct
* Community integrity

xBxBio may act promptly when public content creates a credible privacy, confidentiality, security, or patient-safety concern.

---

## 14. Good-Faith Professional Dialogue

xBxBio welcomes challenging questions and substantive disagreement.

Participants are encouraged to:

* Examine assumptions
* Challenge technical approaches
* Discuss limitations
* Identify risks
* Question evidence
* Propose alternatives
* Identify potential failure modes
* Discuss responsible approaches to emerging technology

The objective is not universal agreement.

The objective is **high-quality professional dialogue that advances responsible cardiovascular technology and clinical intelligence.**
